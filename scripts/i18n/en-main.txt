About the KIK at Home platform
What is KIK at Home?
KIK at Home is a clear and friendly way to support interesting authorial board games.
It is a separate space inside Blue Ferret. Here we raise funds for authorial board game projects that need the community's support. You can browse different projects and see their status, the amount raised and updates.
Form for authors
Browse projects
Why authors choose KIK at Home
We help turn your idea into a full prototype: we find manufacturers, work out the cost of the print run, place the project on the platform and take care of organising production. After a successful fundraiser we send the game into production and you receive the finished print run of your game.
A community around the idea
Real people who love board games and support authors see the projects.
Statuses, amount raised, updates and dates – all public and without extra complications.
Human communication
We guide authors and explain the steps in plain language, without formalities.
Focus on board games
KIK at Home is built specifically for authorial games and their high-quality launch.
