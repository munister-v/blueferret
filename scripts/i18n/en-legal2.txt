This offer is a public proposal to conclude a contract under Articles 633 and 641 of the Civil Code of Ukraine.
You (the “Supporter”) can support a project on the “Support” page. This is a pre-order of a board game that is produced on condition that the fundraising goal is reached. For your support you receive the reward you chose (for example, a copy of the game) after it has been made.
The contract is considered concluded from the moment the invoice is paid (acceptance). A request without payment does not conclude a contract and creates no obligations for either party. A separate signature is not required.
The cost of support is shown on the project page in hryvnias (UAH). VAT is not charged because the Seller is on the simplified taxation system.
Placing a request: the Supporter creates a pre-order request on the Site. At this stage no money is charged to the card and no payment is collected.
If, as a result of the fundraiser (1–5 days before it ends), the project gets the required number of requests / the target amount, the Seller sends the Supporter an electronic invoice or a payment link through the monopay payment service (acquiring by JSC “UNIVERSAL BANK”). The invoice is valid until the final pre-order date. If you do not pay within this period, the request is cancelled.
If, because of unpaid invoices, the amount raised turns out to be lower than the target, the Seller has the right to cancel the project and refund the money paid to the other Supporters in full (section 5).
Acquiring fee. For processing each payment the acquiring bank (monobank) keeps a fee of 1.3% of the payment amount. It is already included in the price, is not added on top and is a cost of the Seller. The bank does not return it to the Seller, but it is not deducted from the Supporter.
For each project, its page shows the target amount and the end date of the fundraiser: e.g. “from 31.11.2026 to 31.12.2026”.
If the goal is not reached, the project is not launched, no invoices are sent and no money is charged. The Seller announces this on the project page and by the Supporter's email.
If the target amount is raised, the Seller makes the game and hands over the rewards under the rules of section 6.

If production fails (the manufacturer refuses, force majeure), the Seller refunds the money paid under section 5, that is, minus the acquiring fee.
A Supporter who is a consumer has the right to withdraw from a distance contract without giving a reason at any time from the day the contract is concluded (payment) and within 14 days of receiving the goods (Article 21 of the Law of Ukraine “On Consumer Rights Protection”), if the goods have not been used and their presentation and packaging are intact. Withdrawal is also possible during production, before the reward is received. If the goods have not been shipped yet, the Seller refunds the full amount to the same card within 14 days of receiving the notice.
The refund procedure is described in detail on the “Refunds” page.
The parties are released from liability for failing to perform their obligations because of force majeure, in particular hostilities, missile attacks, destroyed infrastructure, power or communication outages that make production impossible. The Seller announces such circumstances on the project page. This does not deprive the Supporter of the right to a full refund under sections 5–7.
The Seller's liability is determined by the legislation of Ukraine. Supporters who are consumers are guaranteed the rights provided by the Law “On Consumer Rights Protection”.
